# Kwassont Quest

A Game Boy-style adventure for phones. Mariana and Matias travel through
8 cities: London, Toulouse, Lisbon, Amsterdam, Vienna, Doha, Santiago and Queenstown.
In each city they eat, shop, sightsee and complete a task to earn a passport stamp.

- Pick **Mariana** or **Matias**. Each character has their own save. The other one follows you and joins in.
- Mariana gets tired from walking. Cute things, benches, snacks and coffee give her energy back.
- Matias is always hungry, has a sweet tooth, carries the shopping bags and always needs the toilet.
- Every city has its own song, colour palette, café, restaurant, grocery shop, boutique, sights and locals.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. The on-screen D-pad and A/B/Start/Select buttons work by touch.
On a keyboard: arrows/WASD to move, Z/Space = A, X = B, Enter = Start, Shift = Select (sound on/off).

Files: `engine.js` (screen, font, input, sound, dialogs), `art.js` (sprites and buildings),
`data.js` (songs and cities), `games.js` (mini-games), `world.js` (walking around a city), `main.js` (title, flights, ending).
